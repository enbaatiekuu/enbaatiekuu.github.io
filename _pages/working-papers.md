This page lists ongoing research, manuscripts and selected thesis projects. Items are labelled by their actual stage; a working paper or submitted manuscript should not be interpreted as a published article.

## Working paper

### Central Bank Independence and Inflation Dynamics in Ghana (2002–2023)

This study examines the relationship between central bank independence and inflation dynamics in Ghana. It develops separate de jure and de facto measures of central bank independence using institutional and legal information, including constitutional and ministry-related sources.

**Status:** Working paper.

## Research manuscript

### Insurance Without Delivery: Stage-Specific Constraints in Maternal Healthcare Utilisation — Evidence from Ghana's LEAP 1000 Programme

This research examines whether programme-related health-insurance coverage translates into greater utilisation of skilled and facility-based maternal healthcare services.

**Status:** Research manuscript. For the latest submission status, consult the author before citing.

## Master's thesis

### The Daughter Effect: Gender Preference and Women's Empowerment in India

This thesis uses a difference-in-differences framework and Indian Human Development Survey data to investigate the relationship between the gender of a child and maternal empowerment.

**Status:** Completed master's thesis.

## Undergraduate thesis

### International Trade and Food Productivity

This thesis examined the relationship between international trade and food productivity.

**Status:** Completed undergraduate thesis.
